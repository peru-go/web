Use case: logo-brand. Design one exceptional original tourism identity for PERUGO.PE, a bespoke private travel company in Peru. New direction "Vicuña viajera". The client wants a strong standalone pictorial isotipo with clear Peruvian tourism personality, not just typography. Create a beautifully distilled VICUÑA in confident side profile walking forward: recognizable long graceful neck, two upright ears, compact body, four simplified sturdy legs. Expressive poised silhouette with bold flowing geometry and one subtle negative-space sweep through the body suggesting the movement of an Andean trail. Animal should feel proud, warm, memorable, elegant and approachable, not cute cartoon, not mascot, not realistic illustration. Emblem must be compact, balanced and readable at small size. Design with substantial solid masses and generous negative space. No hair texture, no eye detail, no scenic background. No sun, gear, condor, eagle, wings, circle badge, mountains, suitcase, plane, compass or location pin. Pair with a strong distinctive custom uppercase wordmark exactly "PERUGO.PE", weighty contemporary humanist lettering with crafted character. The .PE is part of the name and clearly legible. Use a refined burnt terracotta #BE553A emblem and dark forest green #233B32 wordmark on fully opaque warm ivory #F4F1E9. One clean landscape identity presentation: large principal horizontal logo with pictorial vicuña left of wordmark occupying main upper area; bottom left the identical isotipo alone at a generous size; bottom right identical full lockup reversed in ivory on a solid forest green rectangle. Only text PERUGO.PE. Make the animal symbol the most memorable aspect. Flat sharp vector-like forms, studio-quality graphic design. Absolutely no shadows, glow, gradients, metallic effects, mockups or transparency. A genuinely considered travel brand, not stock clip art.
